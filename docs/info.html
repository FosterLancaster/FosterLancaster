Who Is Foster Lancaster?

Foster Lancaster is an independent-pop maximalist producer and singer that began as an internet personality and got relatively famous from word of mouth and the wildly popular "fostmp3's platform". His lyrics move audiences, his technology makes people wonder what else is possible, and his look... well you make your own opinion. Foster keeps up with his devoted following via a feature of fostmp3s called "Sup". He mainly answers questions, shares thoughts on his beliefs, and announces upcoming projects.
