Unique Vocalist

Foster Lancaster is a pop singer-songwriter based in Ferndale, Michigan. His non-threatening, articulate, and straight-forward delivery match his pop peers and have taste made a desire for his brand specifically.

Top-Trendsetter

Imitating Foster is becoming the go-to formula for creating successful pop hype. Jealousy and inconvenience camouflage the fact that his audience is more organic than the industry standard representatives.

Constantly Quoted

Whether it’s a joke on his “FostMp3s” widget, or another musical triumph, audiences hear about him constantly. His successful songs like "In Real Life" and "Black Jeans" have given fans a clear understanding of what to expect from a Foster Lancaster song.

Organically Followed

Music is great, but lifestyle and connection are more rare and socially desired. His drive to be a positive role model and his lifestyle transparency have created a small but devoted following.
