Foster Lancaster's Music Career

Who is Foster Lancaster? He's only the definitive artist of this generation. His mellow vocals resonate on the frequency of everyone's perspective. His lyrics inform, challenge, and transform culture. Although his fandom is subtle, it's deeply understood just how meaningful his brand and music are.

He began singing professionally in his late teens. The website RapIndie had shared some of his songs early on and introduced him to a hip hop audience. His prolific personality online over-shadowed much of his early leaked releases, until he created the wildly popular fostmp3s. Foster's music sounds as sincere as it does powerful. His production contrasts every genre and yet feels connected to many different kinds of music.

His most successful album, the Good Link managed to fuse his appeal with his intended message and defined not only who he was, but why it mattered. Since then, he's had the ability to live his vision on a grandiose level and compete with the pop veterans that inspired him.
