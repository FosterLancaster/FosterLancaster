Who Is Foster Lancaster?

Specifically Preferred

The official website of Foster Lancaster, the distinguished internet-pop singer-songwriter. Although many artists imitate him, there is something indescribable that can not be replicated by his peers. Foster is cultured, articulate, intelligent, and comfortable around all people.

Great... Lyricism

There are many pop artists that have personas that overshadow their music. Foster can stand on his discography of highly engaging music as well as his personal brand.

Pop Authenticity

From a very early stage in his career, he was compared to mainstream artists exclusively. Foster's maximalism aesthetic is so air-tight, that people assume he is signed to a major label or was part of a plan.

Passionate Audience

Foster can't do anything privately, people are constantly looking for his latest music, online posts, and stories. People never get tired of keeping up with him.
